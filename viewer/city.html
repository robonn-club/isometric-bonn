How to read it

  • Roofs, ground, trees and heights are measured; wall colours and windows are invented from each building's use.
  • Shadows fall as in the aerial photo, taken around midday on 1 May 2025.
  • The dark line is the city boundary. Neighbouring towns are left blank.

Not there yet

  • Water comes from the photo's infrared; small or green ponds are missed, and bridges are only as good as LoD2.
  • Trees are one surface per canopy.
  • No landmarks or robots drawn by members yet.
